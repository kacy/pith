a required certificate, presented and verified:
    client: hello, pith-client-rsa (issued by pith-client-ca)
    server: ping from pith-client-rsa (issued by pith-client-ca)
a required certificate the client does not have:
    client: refused after the handshake
    server: refused the handshake
an optional certificate the client declines:
    client: hello, an unauthenticated client
    server: ping from an unauthenticated client
a certificate from an issuer the server does not trust:
    client: refused after the handshake
    server: refused the handshake
